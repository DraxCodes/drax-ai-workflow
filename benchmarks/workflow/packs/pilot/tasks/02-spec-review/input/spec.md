# Signup

## Goal

Let a new person create an account with an email address and a password, then
verify the address by clicking a link.

## Flow

1. The person submits an email and password.
2. The server creates the account and sends a verification email.
3. The person clicks the link and the account becomes active.

## Non-goals

Social login is out of scope.

## Success

A new person can sign up and reach the dashboard without contacting support.
