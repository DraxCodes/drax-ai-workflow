# Seat proposals

**Shape.** Option A, a topbar strip. The header is fixed chrome, so the warning cannot be scrolled away and the body does not move.

**Experience.** Option B, a body banner just above the transcript, where the reader is already looking after a send. It must reserve its slot when absent.

**Risk.** Option C is a toast, but a toast is transient and can be missed, so risk rejects it. Risk prefers A or B as long as the state stays visible.

**Chair.** Option A, with the body banner as the close second.

**Dissent.** Experience prefers the body banner. Shape prefers the topbar. Risk rejects the toast.
