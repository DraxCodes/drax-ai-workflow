# Experience brief — test-results table

**Question.** What should the test-results table on a project page feel like at 8/10?

**Fixed point.** The page has a table of 20 test results with name, status and duration. The page must work at 375 and 900.

## Constraints (cannot move)

- No horizontal scroll at 375.
- Status is not colour-only.
- The table does not jump when a row expands.

## Art direction

Quiet, dense, one accent. Status uses a word plus a small mark, not a colour alone.

## Non-goals

Sorting, filtering, export, a chart.
