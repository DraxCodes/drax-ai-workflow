# Experience brief — settings page

**Question.** What should the settings page for the site title and accent colour feel like at 8/10?

**Fixed point.** The admin page has one settings section and a live preview of the site header.

## Constraints (cannot move)

- No save modal, no reload, no layout jump.
- One screen at 375 and 900, light and dark.
- Copy is sentence case; labels are nouns, buttons are verbs.

## Art direction

Quiet, dense, one accent. The accent is the only colour control.

## Non-goals

Per-user settings, theme presets, a settings API.
