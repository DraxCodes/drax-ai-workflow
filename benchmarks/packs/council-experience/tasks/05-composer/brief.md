# Experience brief — the message composer

**Question.** What should the message composer feel like at 8/10?

**Fixed point.** The composer sits at the bottom of the pane. It has a text area, send and attach actions, and a page-local draft.

## Constraints (cannot move)

- No modal; one screen at 375 and 900.
- A half-written draft is never lost on a switch.
- The composer does not move when the first line is typed.

## Art direction

Quiet, dense, one accent. Copy is sentence case; the placeholder is a verb.

## Non-goals

Rich text, slash commands, a send confirmation.
