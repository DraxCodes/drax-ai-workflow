# Experience brief — disconnect a connection

**Question.** What should the disconnect confirmation feel like at 8/10?

**Fixed point.** The pane has one connection. Disconnect stops live updates; a held run may exist.

## Constraints (cannot move)

- One confirmation, not two; no modal stack.
- The connection is named; cancel is the default and holds focus.
- The row stays in place and the list does not jump.

## Art direction

Quiet, dense, one accent. Copy is sentence case, no "Are you sure?".

## Non-goals

Reconnect history, automatic reconnect, a typed confirmation.
